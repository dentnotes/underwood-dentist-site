How it works

A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone where the tooth root used to be. Over a few months it fuses with the bone (osseointegration), creating a stable foundation. A custom crown is then attached on top, matched to your surrounding teeth.

What's involved

  • Initial consultation and digital planning, often with a 3D scan
  • Implant placement, done under local anaesthetic
  • A healing period of 2–4 months before the crown is fitted
Does it hurt?

The placement is done under local anaesthetic, so you shouldn't feel pain — some swelling and tenderness afterward is normal and manageable with standard pain relief.

How long do implants last?

With good oral hygiene, implants can easily last 15–20+ years, often a lifetime.
