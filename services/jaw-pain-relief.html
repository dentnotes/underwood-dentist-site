Common symptoms

Clicking or popping in the jaw joint, pain when chewing, headaches, and a jaw that feels tight or tired — especially on waking — are all common signs of TMJ (temporomandibular joint) dysfunction, often linked to teeth grinding or clenching.

How we help

  • A thorough assessment of your bite and jaw movement
  • A custom night guard or splint to reduce grinding-related strain
  • Referral to a specialist if the case needs more targeted treatment
Is this the same as teeth grinding treatment?

Related, but not identical — grinding is often a cause of jaw pain, so a night guard that protects your teeth can also ease the jaw symptoms.

When should I see someone about jaw pain?

If it's persistent, affecting your sleep or eating, or paired with frequent headaches, it's worth getting assessed rather than just living with it.
