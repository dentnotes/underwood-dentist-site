"We see our patients as unique individuals, not just as a part of a larger picture."

We know it isn't easy to find a dentist you actually trust, especially when work and family already eat up most of your week. So at our Underwood clinic, your concerns get taken seriously, and nobody rushes you out the door.

We treat our local community like family and do everything possible to make sure you're comfortable and confident in the chair — whether that's a routine clean or something you've been putting off for years.

Our promises

Care that fits around your life

A visit to the dentist should feel unhurried, clear and easy to fit into a busy week. These are the things we hold ourselves to at every appointment.

Open seven days

Monday to Friday 8am–6pm, Saturday 9am–4pm and Sunday 10am–4pm, so a check-up never has to wait for a day off.

Same-day emergency care

In pain? We hold same-day slots, weekends included. Call ahead and we'll have the right chair and tools ready when you arrive.

Plain English, no pressure

We explain what we see and what your options are before doing anything, and you never have to decide on the spot.

Same-day crowns

Digital scanning and in-house CEREC milling mean a custom ceramic crown, designed and fitted in a single visit.

Kids welcome, nerves and all

Eligible children are covered under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule, and our team takes things slowly with nervous little ones.

Why people switch to us

A dentist who actually explains things

Most people put off check-ups because of cost, long waits, or a dentist who talks past them. We're trying to be the exception — flexible, upfront about pricing, and happy to answer the "wait, why do I need that?" questions.

  • Appointments that work around school and work hours
  • Plain-English explanations, no pressure to say yes on the spot
For the whole family

Even nervous kids come around

Our team is good with children who genuinely don't want to be there — calm, patient, and quick to explain things in terms that actually make sense to a six-year-old. Good habits started early tend to stick.

  • Bulk-billed for eligible kids under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule
  • Gentle, age-appropriate numbing for fillings and extractions
