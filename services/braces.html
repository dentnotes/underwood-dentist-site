Why braces, not aligners

Fixed braces give more precise control over tooth movement, which makes them the better option for significant crowding, large gaps, or bite corrections that clear aligners can't fully address.

What's involved

  • Brackets are bonded to each tooth and connected with a wire
  • The wire is adjusted roughly every 4–6 weeks to guide teeth into position
  • A retainer is worn afterward to hold the result
Do braces hurt?

Some pressure and tenderness for a few days after each adjustment is normal, but it's manageable and settles quickly.

How long is treatment?

Typically 12–24 months, depending on the complexity of the correction.
