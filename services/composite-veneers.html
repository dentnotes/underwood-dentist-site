How it's different to porcelain

Composite veneers use the same tooth-coloured resin as a filling, sculpted directly onto your tooth and hardened with a curing light — no lab, no second visit. They cost less than porcelain, but aren't quite as stain-resistant or long-lasting.

How long do composite veneers last?

Typically 5–7 years, and they can usually be touched up or repaired rather than fully replaced.

Can I switch to porcelain later?

Yes — composite veneers are a reasonable way to trial a new smile shape before committing to porcelain.
