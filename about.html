About the practice

We see patients as people, not appointment slots

Dentist at Underwood is a family dental practice in Logan City. We treat our local community like family — and do everything we can to make sure you're comfortable and confident, even in the chair.

"We see our patients as unique individuals, not just as a part of a larger picture."

If you or your family have experienced a lot of dental anxiety, we want to change your perception of the dentist. People just want to be valued, appreciated and included — even when they're sitting in the dentist's chair.

We serve residents from South Brisbane, Logan, Underwood, Rochedale, Springwood, Slacks Creek, Eight Mile Plains, Runcorn and the surrounding suburbs of Brisbane's southern region.

Meet the team

The dentists at Underwood

Dr Raghed Bashour

Dentist

Dr Raghed Bashour brings extensive experience in implant, aesthetic and laser dentistry, along with digital smile design, CAD/CAM same-day restorations, digital scanning and implant planning. Since graduating as a Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2010, he has continued advanced education in implant, aesthetic and laser dentistry and became an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry in 2016.

Dr Bashour has worked across south-east Queensland and has experience coordinating complex dental care under deep sedation and general anaesthesia. He enjoys using modern technology to restore both function and appearance, while building long-term relationships with his patients. Dr Bashour speaks English and Arabic.

Dr Varadharaj Kengaiha (Raj)

Dentist

Dr Varadharaj Kengaiha, known as Raj, was inspired to become a dentist after seeing the transformative effect of oral surgery on a close family member. He is committed to continual learning and takes a strong interest in modern dental technologies, materials and clinical techniques.

Raj enjoys every aspect of dentistry, from the technical detail of treatment to helping patients regain confidence in their smiles. His calm, reassuring approach is especially valuable for people who arrive feeling apprehensive or embarrassed, and he works alongside each patient throughout their treatment journey.

Dr Mustafa Al-Arkee

Dentist

Dr Mustafa Al-Arkee is an Australian-registered dentist with more than nine years of clinical experience in general dentistry, periodontology and implant dentistry. He holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery and a Master of Clinical Dentistry in Periodontology and Implantology from the Medical University of Vienna.

His advanced interests include gum-disease management, periodontal surgery, soft-tissue grafting, guided bone regeneration, bone augmentation, dental implant placement and restoration, and management of peri-implant conditions. He also provides restorative dentistry, crowns and bridges, root canal treatment and oral rehabilitation. Dr Al-Arkee is known for his calm manner, attention to detail and evidence-based, patient-centred care.

Dr Ahmed Abdulmunem

Dentist

Dr Ahmed Abdulmunem is a caring and detail-oriented dentist who graduated from the University of Queensland with First Class Honours in Dental Science. He enjoys all aspects of general dentistry, with particular interests in restorative dentistry, oral surgery and endodontics.

Known for his calm and approachable manner, Dr Ahmed believes great dentistry starts with listening, clear communication and helping patients feel comfortable throughout their treatment. Fluent in both English and Arabic, he enjoys caring for patients from all backgrounds.

Outside the clinic, Dr Ahmed has a passion for innovation and education, having worked as a Head Anatomy Demonstrator at the University of Queensland and founded Dentnotes, a digital platform designed to improve dental workflows.

Dr Omel Qalandar

Dentist · BDt (Class 1 Hons), BDSc, Master of Dentistry (Griffith University)

Dr Omel Qalandar brings a dedicated, detail-oriented approach to modern dentistry, combining high-quality clinical expertise with a genuine focus on patient comfort. As an experienced practitioner, Dr Omel values precise, evidence-based care, utilising trusted clinical systems and modern techniques to deliver seamless treatment across general, restorative and aesthetic procedures.

Driven by a passion for gentle, personal care, Dr Omel understands that visiting the dentist can feel daunting for many. She takes the time to listen, explain options clearly and tailor every treatment plan to suit the individual, ensuring patients feel relaxed, supported and fully informed at every step.

Outside the clinic, Dr Omel is an avid traveller who loves exploring new destinations, discovering great food spots and spending quality time with her family.
