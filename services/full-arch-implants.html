Full Arch Implants

A fixed, implant-supported replacement for a full arch of missing or failing teeth.

Book an appointment Or call (07) 3822 9266

How it works

Rather than placing an implant for every single tooth, a full arch of replacement teeth is supported by a smaller number of strategically placed implants (commonly four to six) — a fixed bridge is then attached, giving you a full set of teeth that doesn't come out.

Who it's for

This is typically for people who are missing most or all of the teeth in an arch, or who have failing teeth that are better replaced than repeated restored one at a time.

Is it the same as dentures?

No — unlike removable dentures, a full-arch implant solution is fixed in place. It functions much more like natural teeth, with significantly better chewing strength.

How long does the whole process take?

Planning, placement and healing typically spans several months — we'll give you a realistic timeline at your consultation.
