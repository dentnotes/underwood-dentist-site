Wrinkle Treatments

Anti-wrinkle injectable treatments, delivered by a dentist with a detailed understanding of facial anatomy.

Why see a dentist for this

Dentists spend years studying the detailed anatomy of the face, jaw and muscles involved in expression — which translates directly into precise, conservative injectable treatment for fine lines around the mouth, eyes and forehead.

What's involved

A consultation first, to talk through what you're hoping to achieve and whether it's a good fit for you, followed by a quick in-chair treatment with minimal downtime.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as a quick pinch rather than real pain — a topical numbing option is available if you're needle-shy.

How long do results last?

Typically three to four months, after which a top-up treatment maintains the result.
