How it works

A digital scan of your teeth is used to map out a series of custom aligners, each one shifting your teeth slightly closer to their final position. You wear each set for about a week or two before moving to the next, swapping them out roughly every 20–22 hours a day.

Who it suits

Clear aligners work well for mild to moderate crowding, gaps and bite issues. More complex cases may still need traditional braces — we'll let you know honestly which applies to you.

Can I eat and drink normally?

Yes — aligners come out for eating and drinking, so there are no food restrictions like there are with fixed braces.

How long does treatment take?

Typically 6–18 months depending on how much movement is needed.
