Your Trusted Underwood Dentist in Logan City

We know it’s not easy to find a dentist you can trust, especially when it’s hard to make time between work, family, and everything else. We also understand how important it is to be heard and treated with real care. At our Underwood dental clinic, you’re treated like a valude member of our family, and take your concerns seriously.

Focusing on all areas of dentistry, including general, emergency, restorative and cosmetic dentistry, Dentist at Underwood can help you and your family enhance the beauty and functionality of your smiles. Whether you need a simple check-up and clean, a filling, orthodontics or surgery, we’re a dedicated Underwood dentist who cares about your oral and overall health. 

A Dentist on Brisbane Southside Who Cares

Most experts recommend you visit the dentist every six months for a check-up and clean. However, there are many people who visit a lot less. Many people cite expensive costs, long waiting times, and dentists who don’t listen or communicate clearly as the main reasons their experience of going to the dentist has soured.

As an Underwood dentist with a caring, affordable and flexible approach, this is a perception we’re striving to change. We’re a community-minded dentist in the Logan city region that provides a warm and welcoming environment for both children and adults in the Logan area.

We offer flexible appointments that can work around your family’s school and work hours, so you don’t have to miss out on visiting the dentist due to your busy schedule.

Family-Friendly Dentist

Our family-friendly team and soothing, comfortable environment help even children who struggle with dental anxiety relax and feel at ease in our clinic. We build rapport with kids easily and help soothe their fears, explaining even complex dental terms in straightforward ways they can understand.

Our children’s dentistry service helps children become accustomed to visiting the dentist from an early age and encourages positive habits that support good oral health throughout their lives.

To make treatments like fillings or tooth extractions less frightening for children, we offer local anaesthetics designed to keep them comfortable.

  • Our children’s dentistry treatments are covered under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) for eligible children and teens up to age 17.

Flexible Payment Options

Making dental care more affordable

We understand that procedures like dental implants can come with a high cost. That’s why we aim to make high-quality dental care more accessible for all patients.

At Dentist at Underwood, we strive to make high-quality dental care accessible to everyone. We accept Afterpay, Zip, TLC and Pretty Penny Finance, giving you flexible payment options that suit your budget.
