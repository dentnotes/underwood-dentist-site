Your Trusted Underwood Dentist in Logan City

A Different Kind of Visit

A Dentist on Brisbane's Southside Who Actually Listens

Most experts recommend a check-up every six months — yet many people put it off. Expensive costs, long waits, and dentists who don't really listen are the most common reasons the experience has soured for so many.

As a caring, affordable and flexible Underwood dentist, we're working to change that perception — with a warm, welcoming environment for children and adults alike.

  • Flexible appointments around school & work hours
  • Clear, honest explanations — no pressure
Family-Friendly

Helping Kids Feel at Ease, From Their Very First Visit

Our team and calm, comfortable clinic help even children with dental anxiety relax. We build rapport quickly and explain things in simple, friendly terms they can understand.

Starting good habits early supports a lifetime of healthy teeth — and treatments like fillings or extractions are made as comfortable as possible for little patients.

  • Covered under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS)
  • Gentle, age-appropriate local anaesthetics
