Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment saves a tooth that would otherwise need to be removed — and gets rid of the pain for good.

Book an appointment Or call (07) 3822 9266

Why it's needed

When the soft tissue inside a tooth (the pulp) becomes infected or inflamed — usually from deep decay or a crack — it needs to be removed to stop the pain and save the tooth.

Without treatment, the infection can spread, and the tooth usually has to be extracted.

What's involved

  • The infected pulp is removed and the canal is cleaned and shaped
  • The space is sealed to prevent reinfection
  • A crown is usually fitted afterward to protect the weakened tooth
Is root canal treatment painful?

Done under local anaesthetic, the procedure itself shouldn't hurt — most people find it no worse than getting a filling. It's the infection beforehand that's painful, not the treatment.

What happens if I just have the tooth pulled instead?

That's an option, but it leaves a gap that can shift your other teeth over time — a root canal keeps your natural tooth in place.
